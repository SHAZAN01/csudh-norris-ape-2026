\section{Development Conclusion and Remaining Evaluation}
The completed pilot shows why post-editing evaluation must track preservation and operational validity alongside mean quality. GPT one-shot provides a positive English-to-Tagalog development estimate, while strict reason-then-edit validation exposes a substantial Llama compliance limitation. Case inspection shows that metric gains can accompany factual corruption and that large metric losses need not imply equally clear meaning loss.

These observations guide protocol review; they do not answer the four research questions conclusively. The next stages are to resolve the documented overlap and output-policy decisions, record the frozen configuration, execute the held-out matrix, and integrate the prespecified comparative analysis. Final results and conclusions will be revised from that evidence rather than projected from this pilot.
