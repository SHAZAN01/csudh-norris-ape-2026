\section{Limitations}
The completed study is a 100-ID development pilot. Repeated model/strategy outputs are dependent, and paired directions share semantic content. Development intervals are descriptive after prompt development; they are not held-out confirmation. Two language pairs cannot identify a causal effect of language-resource availability. Baseline quality differs across directions, and some quality strata are sparse.

FLORES+ sources largely originate in English, so reverse directions involve translated source text and can exhibit translationese effects. This further limits a causal interpretation of directional asymmetry. Its independent target references are not minimal human post-edits, and neither editing effort nor user productivity is measured.

One open-weight checkpoint and one dated commercial snapshot do not represent all LLM families, scales, or contemporary models. Pretraining exposure to the public benchmark cannot be ruled out. Deterministic decoding and pinned model IDs improve traceability without guaranteeing identical remote behavior. Independent fragment translation can lose cross-sentence context.

COMET and heuristic flags are imperfect proxies; no independent bilingual annotation was collected. The source-based case review illustrates mechanisms rather than population error rates. The independent audit verifies numeric provenance and aggregation, not a fresh neural scoring run. The ICAI comparison uses its supplied final manuscript, without a byte-level audit of its unavailable code or data. Main statistical contrasts, multiplicity handling, and protocol freeze remain to be finalized before devtest evaluation.
