\section{Discussion}
\textbf{What separates the editors is not how often they edit.} Both editors change most drafts under one-shot prompting, but GPT-4o Mini's changes to strong drafts are rarely harmful, whereas Llama's frequently are. Its losses concentrate where S0 is already good: in the protect pool, Llama's mean change ranges from $-0.33$ to $-2.09$ points across conditions, while its improve-pool changes are positive in 11 of 12 conditions. A post-editor is therefore only useful if it leaves correct drafts alone.

\textbf{Explicit reasoning did not help.} Reason-then-edit was never significantly better than zero-shot or one-shot, its point estimate was lower in all 16 such contrasts, and for Llama it was the most harmful strategy in every direction. The development pilot showed why this must be measured with a valid contract: under the original strict contract, 227 of 400 Llama reason-then-edit outputs fell back to the baseline, hiding the over-editing that the revised, fully valid condition exposes on held-out data. Diagnoses are model outputs, not verified explanations.

\textbf{Preservation screens.} Heuristic screens flag few items relative to S0: at most 23 outputs per condition change a digit (Llama zero-shot En$\to$Tl), and language-ID mismatches rise above the baseline mainly for Llama reason-then-edit Tl$\to$En (10 vs.\ 1).
