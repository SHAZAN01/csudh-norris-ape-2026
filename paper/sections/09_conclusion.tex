\section{Conclusion}
We evaluated LLM post-editing of a strong, sentence-split NLLB-200 baseline for English--Tagalog and English--Spanish in both directions, with two editors, three prompting strategies, a protocol frozen before test access, and explicit accounting for failures and over-editing. \pending{three sentences answering RQ1--RQ4 from the held-out evidence}. Average gains, preservation of strong drafts, and output validity are separate properties, and an LLM post-editor should be assessed on all three before deployment. Future work should add bilingual human assessment, more language pairs, and document-level context.
