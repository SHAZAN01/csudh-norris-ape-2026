\section{Conclusion}
On held-out FLORES+ data, LLM post-editing of a strong, sentence-split NLLB-200 baseline helped or harmed depending above all on the editor. GPT-4o Mini improved every direction, most for the Tagalog pair, where weak drafts are more frequent, and most with one retrieved exemplar; Llama 3 8B never improved and over-edited strong drafts, especially with reason-then-edit. Gains came from weak drafts, direction effects were small, and explicit diagnosis did not improve either editor. Average gains, preservation of strong drafts, and output validity are separate properties that should all be measured before deployment. Future work should add bilingual human assessment, more language pairs and editors, and document-level context.
